# Vidya Orbit — Brand identity

## Brand idea
**One clear orbit for the work of running a learning centre.** Vidya Orbit makes centre operations feel organised, understandable and supported—not more complicated.

## Personality
Clear, capable, warm, modern, dependable. Speak in plain English. Prefer concrete benefits over hype. Avoid claims about outcomes, scale or performance unless independently verified.

## Logo system
- **Primary logo:** `vidya-orbit-logo.svg` — wordmark with orbital mark.
- **Standalone mark:** `vidya-orbit-mark.svg` — use for avatars, app icons and small placements.
- The ring represents connected centre operations; the blue planet is the learning hub; the orange satellite is a small cue for momentum and support.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the satellite diameter. Do not stretch, rotate, add effects, or place on a visually noisy background.
- On dark backgrounds, use the mark with a white wordmark; do not recolor the blue core or orange satellite without a specific contrast need.

## Color palette
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Orbit Blue | `#1746E8` |
| Accent | Signal Orange | `#F05A28` |
| Ink | Near Black | `#111111` |
| Paper | Warm White | `#F8F7F2` |
| Secondary text | Slate | `#52545A` |

Use warm white as the default canvas, ink for copy, blue for primary emphasis, and orange sparingly for a key accent. Keep contrast high.

## Typography
- **Display:** Space Grotesk Bold (fallback: Arial Bold). Use large, direct headlines with compact line breaks.
- **Body:** DM Sans (fallback: Arial). Keep supporting copy calm, readable and short.
- Use sentence case for headlines and labels. Avoid all-caps body copy and excessive font weights.

## Layout principles
- Swiss/editorial grid; align to a consistent left edge.
- One dominant headline or price per composition.
- Divide content into a few clearly labeled groups; use thin rules and ample whitespace.
- Geometric orbital motif is a supporting signature, not decoration on every surface.
- Digital posters: 1080 × 1350 px (4:5). Keep text within 64 px safe margins.

## Voice examples
- Prefer: “Track fees clearly.”
- Prefer: “We set up the system for your team.”
- Avoid: “Revolutionary AI-powered transformation.”
- Avoid pressure language, unsupported guarantees, invented testimonials or statistics.

## Poster set
