**How to read type and status**

- **Confirmed defect** — concrete evidence in repo, live export, public GET, or a local probe.
- **Suspected risk** — plausible from code, not reproduced end-to-end.
- **Unverified** — needs a live JWT or a signed-in session. Function bodies and triggers are no longer in this bucket.
- **Verified OK** — inspected and closed; not a defect.
- **Planned change** — queued work, not a defect.
- **Confirmed** in Status means the *finding* is confirmed, not that a live exploit was run.
- **P0** is reserved for data exposure, cross-institute leakage, or corruption. None confirmed after the function/trigger export.
